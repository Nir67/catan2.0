# Card artwork

Original illustration atlas generated with the built-in ImageGen tool for this project. The atlas has five resource illustrations in its top row (wood, brick, wool, grain, ore) and five development illustrations in the bottom row (knight, victory point, road building, year of plenty, monopoly). Card frames and Hebrew typography are rendered in HTML/CSS to stay sharp and accessible.

Asset: `src/assets/cards/card-art.png`
Consumers: `src/components/Cards.tsx` and `src/components/Cards.css`

## Final generation prompt

Create ONE production-ready game illustration texture atlas for a premium physical Euro-style island settlement board game. A precisely aligned 5-column by 2-row grid, all TEN cells identical size and exact borders at multiples of 20% width and 50% height. Landscape overall aspect ratio 5:3, ideally 2000x1200 or 2500x1500, so each cell is a portrait 2:3 illustration. NO gutters, NO white margins, NO rounded corners, NO border frames, NO lettering, NO typography, NO numbers, NO icons, NO emoji, NO logos, NO watermark. Artwork fills each cell edge-to-edge. Each cell is a full lush narrative painting, not a single isolated object or sticker. Consistent realistic hand-painted vintage board-game illustration, detailed brushwork, rich believable materials, natural cinematic light, Mediterranean late medieval world, warm golden parchment color grading, attractive clear silhouettes readable at thumbnail scale. Ten DISTINCT paintings, exactly this reading order left-to-right: TOP ROW 1 timber: freshly cut logs and a woodcutter's axe at a sunlit pine forest sawmill; 2 brick: stacks of realistic red terracotta bricks outside a traditional brick kiln; 3 wool: fluffy sheep and rolls of cream fleece in a lush pastoral meadow; 4 grain: ripe golden wheat sheaves in a sweeping field with an old windmill; 5 ore: rough iron ore rocks and a pickaxe at a rocky mountain mine, blue gray tones. BOTTOM ROW 1 knight: noble armored knight on horseback guarding the island coast, realistic dignified medieval armor; 2 victory: flourishing fortified medieval harbor city under golden light, laurel branches in foreground; 3 road building: workers building a stone-paved road and small stone bridge crossing green hills; 4 year of plenty: abundant harvest baskets overflowing with wheat, wool, timber and vegetables outside a farm; 5 monopoly: wealthy medieval merchant at a trading table with resource crates and ledgers in a bustling market. Every painting must be unique. Keep important subjects in upper-middle 75% of each portrait cell; the lower quarter can be quiet scenic foreground to allow UI labels to overlay later. This is a SINGLE seamless rectangular atlas file used directly with CSS background-position, therefore exact grid alignment and consistent cell geometry are critical.
